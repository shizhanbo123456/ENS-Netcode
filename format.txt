每次会把下列各语句合并，并用*分隔，一起发送

(byte messageHeader)(ushort delivery){body}(2byte messageLength)
messageLength有特殊编码
messageType和messageLength都必定不包含Separator


Body部分消息结构

E:
>>客户端接收                事件
(byte eventId)(short clientId)                                 //eventId    ==1 OnClientConnect  ==2 OnClientDisconnect

C:
>>客户端接收                分配Id
(short Clientid)

A:
>>服务器接收
(short clientId)         设置房主权限

>>客户端接收
(bool authorityActive)


F:
>>服务器接收                函数调用
(short NOMObjectId)(byte func)(bytes param)(short target)      //param为原始参数字节，长度与格式由 func 决定

>>客户端接收                函数调用
(short NOMObjectId)(byte func)(bytes param)                    //param为原始参数字节，长度与格式由 func 决定


f:
>>服务器接收                创建物体
(short sendto)(bool false)(short collectionId)(string param)(short behaviourCount)
(short sendto)(bool true)(short collectionId)(string param)(short idStart)

>>客户端接收                创建物体
(bool false)(short collectionId)(string param)(short idStart)
(bool true)(short collectionId)(string param)(short idStart)


H:
>>服务器接收                心跳检测
(int millisecondInServerMsg)

>>客户端接收                心跳检测
(int serverMillisecond)(int delay)
